# Domain Knowledge - Sun Life Philippines

## Business Domain
Sun Life Philippines — the country's **#1 life insurance company** (by premium income per IC data as of December 2024), operating since the late 19th century. A subsidiary of Sun Life Financial, a leading Canadian international financial services organization. Regulated by the **Insurance Commission of the Philippines (IC)** and the **Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)** for mutual fund operations.

---

## Core Business Lines

### 1. Insurance Products

#### Life Insurance (Personal)
- **Term Insurance**: Pure protection with no savings component; fixed coverage periods; affordable premiums
  - SUN Safer Life, SUN Healthier Life, Sun LifeAssure, Sun Maiden/Plus, Sun First Aid/Plus
- **Wealth Transfer (Participating / Traditional)**: Combines protection with guaranteed cash values and dividends; coverage to age 100
  - Sun Life Premier Legacy, SUN Smarter Life Classic, Sun StartUp, Sun Life Save and Protect
- **Wealth Accumulation (VUL)**: Variable Unit-Linked products — insurance + investment in mutual funds; coverage to age 88 or 100
  - Sun MaxiLink series, Sun FlexiLink series, Sun Dream Achiever, Sun Acceler8, Sun Legacy, Sun Wealth Prime 7, Sun Life Secure Income, SUN Smarter Life Elite

#### Health Insurance
- Critical illness, hospitalization, and wellness plans
- Products: SUN Cancer Care, SUN Fit and Well, SUN Senior Care, Sun ICU Protect, Sun Life OFW Health Protect

#### Group Life Insurance
- Employer-sponsored group coverage; overview at /en/insurance/group-insurance/

#### Business Owners Insurance Packages
- Bundled insurance for entrepreneurs and SME owners

#### Digital Insurance (D.E.A.L.)
- Group insurance purchased digitally through partner platforms
- Life Armor: customizable accident plan, buy online

#### Investment-Linked Insurance (VUL overview)
- /en/insurance/VULs-and-Fund-Options/
- **NAVPU** (Net Asset Value per Unit): VUL fund unit price; published daily
- **Fund Value**: total value of units allocated to a policy
- **Fund Allocation**: percentage split of premiums across different investment funds

### 2. Investment Products (Sun Life Prosperity Funds)

Managed by **Sun Life Asset Management Company, Inc. (SLAMCI)** — SEC-regulated investment company.
Products are **mutual funds** that pool investor capital for diversified portfolios.

**Risk Profile Tiers:**

| Profile | Risk Level | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative Investor | Very Low | Capital preservation; stable returns; money market/bond funds |
| Moderate Investor | Low | Capital stability with modest income; minimal volatility |
| Balanced Investor | Medium | Mix of income and capital growth; accepts some volatility |
| Growth Investor | High | Long-term capital growth; substantial risk tolerance |
| Aggressive Investor | Very High | Maximum returns potential; accepts significant volatility |

**Key Investment URLs:**
- Funds list: /en/investments/sun-life-prosperity-funds/
- Online investing: https://online.sunlife.com.ph/cdt/esales/isa
- SLAMCI contact: SUNLINK Client Care — (+632) 8849-9888 | sunlink@sunlife.com

**Investment Concepts:**
- **NAVPS** (Net Asset Value per Share): price per mutual fund share
- **Forward pricing**: next computed NAVPS used for buy/sell transactions
- **Redemption**: selling back mutual fund shares; processed within 7 business days
- **Settlement Bank Account (SBA)**: bank account enrolled for redemption payouts

## Key Business Concepts & Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Policy Owner** | Person who owns and pays for the insurance policy |
| **Life Insured** | Person whose life is covered by the policy |
| **Beneficiary** | Person designated to receive the death benefit |
| **Premium** | Regular payment made to keep the policy active |
| **Face Amount** | The death benefit amount stated in the policy |
| **Cash Value / CSV** | Accumulated savings portion of a life insurance policy |
| **Surrender** | Terminating a policy early in exchange for its CSV |
| **Reinstatement** | Reactivating a lapsed policy |
| **Grace Period** | Extra time (typically 31 days) to pay overdue premiums |
| **VUL** | Variable Unit-Linked — insurance + investment product |
| **NAVPU** | Net Asset Value per Unit — VUL fund unit price |
| **NAVPS** | Net Asset Value per Share — mutual fund share price |
| **Dividend** | Profit distribution in participating insurance policies |
| **Participating Insurance** | Policy that participates in insurer's profits via dividends |
| **Term Insurance** | Pure protection; no cash value; terminates at end of term |
| **Wealth Transfer** | Products designed to pass wealth to next generation |
| **Wealth Accumulation** | Products designed to grow wealth over time |
| **Critical Illness** | Specified serious conditions (cancer, heart attack, stroke, etc.) |
| **Riders** | Optional add-ons to base insurance policies |
| **Top-up** | Additional investment premium on a VUL policy |
| **Policy Advance / Loan** | Borrowing against a policy's cash value |
| **ePolicy** | Electronic digital version of the policy contract |
| **D.E.A.L.** | Digital Engagement and Acquisition Link — digital group insurance |

## Regulatory Context

- **Insurance Commission (IC)**: Regulates Sun Life Assurance Company of Canada (Philippines branch)
  - IC hotline referenced on product pages for consumer concerns
- **Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)**: Regulates SLAMCI (mutual funds arm)
  - SEC-CGFD contact: +63 2 8818-5952 / email CGFD@sec.gov.ph
- **Every product page** ends with IC disclaimer: "The above information is a product summary for reference only..."
- **Insurance Commissioner approval** required for all products sold in the Philippines
