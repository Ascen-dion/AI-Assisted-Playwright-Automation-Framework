# Experian — Domain Context

## Business Domain
Experian is one of the three major consumer credit bureaus in the United States (along with Equifax and TransUnion). The company operates in the **consumer credit reporting, identity protection, and financial services** domain.

## Core Products & Services

### Credit & Scores
- **Free Credit Report** — consumers can access their Experian credit report at no cost
- **FICO® Score** — provides FICO® Score 8 (may differ from lender-used versions)
- **Experian Boost®** — allows consumers to add utility, phone, and streaming payments to improve scores
- **Credit Monitoring** — real-time alerts on credit file changes
- **3-Bureau Reports** — combined Experian + Equifax + TransUnion reports

### Identity Protection
- **CreditLock** — instant lock/unlock of Experian credit file
- **Identity Theft Protection** — monitoring and resolution services
- **Security Freeze** — legally mandated free credit freeze
- **Fraud Alerts** — flag on credit file warning lenders

### Financial Tools
- **Experian Smart Money™** — digital checking account with credit-building features
- **Bill Negotiator** — helps reduce monthly bills
- **Personal Loans marketplace** — comparison shopping for loans
- **Credit Card offers** — No Ding Decline™ (soft pull only)
- **Insurance comparison** — auto, home insurance quotes

## Key Domain Terms
| Term                  | Meaning                                               |
|-----------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| FICO® Score           | Credit score model developed by Fair Isaac Corporation |
| Credit Report         | Detailed record of credit history from a bureau        |
| Credit Freeze         | Legal right to restrict access to credit file          |
| Fraud Alert           | Note on credit file requiring extra verification       |
| Experian Boost®       | Tool to add non-traditional payment data to file       |
| No Ding Decline™      | Credit card matching with no hard inquiry              |
| CreditLock            | Experian's proprietary instant lock feature            |
| Three-Bureau          | Experian, Equifax, TransUnion collectively             |

## Regulatory Notes
- Credit freezes are federally mandated and free
- FICO® is a registered trademark of Fair Isaac Corporation
- Experian must comply with FCRA (Fair Credit Reporting Act)
- Privacy governed by U.S. data privacy policy and state-specific laws

## Target Audience
- **Consumers** — individuals checking credit, protecting identity, managing finances
- **Small Business** — business credit reports, customer credit profiles
- **Enterprise** — decisioning software, fraud management, healthcare/mortgage solutions
